Good to know
Name, Class, Date of Birth and Gender are always required — Schoolzen needs them to create the student record itself.
Hiding a field only affects new admissions — it doesn't remove data already saved for existing students.
Add a custom field for anything your school tracks that isn't listed — with its own type and validation rules.
State-specific fields (like Samagra ID) follow your school's state, set in School Profile — only the ones for your state ever show here.
