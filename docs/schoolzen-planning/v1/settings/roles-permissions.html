Roles & Permissions

Two steps: set up what each role can do, then say who holds it for which class.
Step 1 — What can each role do?
Pick a role, then switch on whatever it should be allowed to do. The sidebar on the left previews what that role would see — locked items are modules it has no View access to.
Super Admin always has full access to everything and can't be changed — every school needs one role that can never be locked out.
This role can be limited to specific classes — set per staff member in Step 2 below, since different people with this role may teach different classes.
Remove this role
Step 2 — Who covers which class?
Every staff member as a row, every role as its own column — click a chip to edit it, or the + to add one.

Good to know
Step 1 sets what a role can do. Step 2 sets which class each person with that role actually covers.
The same class + section can't be given to two people for the same role.
A role can't be removed while staff still hold it — reassign them first.
Click any chip to edit its class, or the × to remove it. The dotted + adds another role without disturbing what they already hold.
